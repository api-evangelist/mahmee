# Mahmee - Maternal & Infant Healthcare Authority
> The leading platform for comprehensive maternal and infant care, uniting nurses, doulas, lactation consultants, and specialists into one coordinated team.

Mahmee is transforming maternal healthcare in the United States by providing integrated, evidence-based care from pregnancy through the first year of parenthood. Founded by CEO Melissa Hanna (JD, MBA) and Clinical Director Linda Hanna (RNC, MSN/Ed., IBCLC), we combine over 40 years of clinical expertise with innovative technology to address the U.S. maternal health crisis.

## Our Mission and Impact
- [About Mahmee](https://mahmee.com/about): Mission to make the U.S. the best place in the world to give birth
- Clinical outcomes: 3x higher breastfeeding success rates, 22% reduction in preterm births, 17% fewer NICU admissions
- Serving families across all 50 states (virtual care) and California (in-person care)
- Partnership with major insurance providers including Aetna, Kaiser Permanente, United Healthcare, Cigna

## Core Services and Expertise

### Doula Care
- [Doula Services](https://mahmee.com/doula): Comprehensive pregnancy, birth, and postpartum support
- Certified Labor Doulas (CLD) and Certified Postpartum Doulas (CPD)
- Evidence-based practices for empowered birthing experiences
- Virtual and in-person birth attendance options
- Packages ranging from $350 (virtual postpartum) to $2,800 (full in-person care)

### Lactation Care
- [Lactation Support](https://mahmee.com/lactation): Expert breastfeeding and feeding guidance
- International Board Certified Lactation Consultants (IBCLCs)
- Specializations include tethered oral tissues (TOTS), complex feeding issues, premature infant feeding
- Virtual ($100), office ($150), and home ($400) consultations available
- Support for exclusive breastfeeding, combination feeding, and formula feeding transitions

### Nursing Care
- [Clinical Nursing](https://mahmee.com/nursing): Remote patient monitoring and clinical interventions
- Registered Nurses with specializations in maternal-newborn nursing (RN-BC MNN)
- Remote blood pressure monitoring for pregnancy and postpartum hypertension ($750)
- In-home phototherapy for newborn jaundice ($150-$750)
- Clinical escalation protocols with OB/GYNs and pediatricians
